# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Any business that needs an online presence, or needs to reshape the one it has, and wants to rank higher in search to get better results. Broad by intent: from small businesses to growth-stage brands. They arrive deciding whether to hire a studio to build or rebuild their site.

## Product Purpose

Optflow is a design agency that builds brands, products, campaigns and high-performance web platforms designed for organic growth. The marketing site exists to win client inquiries; success is a visitor starting a conversation (the site's closing CTA: "Let's build a website that drives real business results").

## Positioning

A design-led studio with a performance edge: premium craft first, with search visibility and measurable results as the proof. Design quality is the entry point; performance and organic growth are what a pure-aesthetic studio could not truthfully claim.

## Operating Context

Single-page agency site with sections for hero, clients, services, work, process, pricing, testimonials, journal (articles on web design, performance and organic search), FAQ and a closing CTA. The methodology is presented as "Our methodology" and pricing as "Transparent Investment" with packages tied to business ROI.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services described in copy: brand, product and campaign design; high-performance web platforms; SEO/organic search; end-to-end capabilities.
- Existing stack: Astro 7 with framer-motion, lenis (smooth scroll), three.js and lucide-react; custom canvas scripts (grid-wave, pixel-cloud, particle-network).
- Exact service list, package contents and pricing are not confirmed and are placeholder copy.

## Evidence on Hand

All current content is placeholder: client names, testimonials, case studies, pricing, metrics and journal articles. Future work must not present any of it as fact and must not invent new clients, results, benchmarks or testimonials; use clearly neutral placeholder content until the user supplies real material.

## Product Principles

1. Craft is the credential: the site itself must demonstrate the quality it sells.
2. Performance is part of the design: speed, crawlability and search-readiness are never traded away for effect.
3. Claims need proof: no result, client or number appears without real evidence behind it.
4. Speak to any business: clear for a small-business owner, credible to a marketing lead.
